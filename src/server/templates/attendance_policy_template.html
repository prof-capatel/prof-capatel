Complete Employee Attendance & Punctuality Policy Template (2026)

A standardized, comprehensive company attendance policy ready to adopt for Indian startups, SMEs, and corporate enterprises. Covers biometric facial recognition check-in, GPS geofencing, grace periods, leave workflows, and salary deduction rules.

[COMPANY NAME] — EMPLOYEE ATTENDANCE, PUNCTUALITY & TIMEKEEPING POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Version 2.4 • Applicable To: All Permanent, Probationary & Contractual Staff
1. Purpose & Scope

This Attendance and Punctuality Policy establishes uniform standards for employee work hours, daily timekeeping, late arrival thresholds, and leave synchronization. Consistent attendance is essential for maintaining productivity, fair shift allocations, and error-free monthly payroll calculations.

2. Standard Working Hours & Shifts

The standard workweek comprises 48 hours distributed across designated operating shifts:

  • General Shift: 09:30 AM – 06:30 PM (with a 60-minute combined lunch & tea recess).
  • Morning Shift: 07:00 AM – 03:30 PM (Operational / Production units).
  • Evening Shift: 03:00 PM – 11:30 PM.
3. Biometric Facial Recognition Check-In Procedure

To eliminate manual sign-in errors and buddy-punching, [Company Name] utilizes the Curiosity HUB Cloud Face Recognition System:

  • All employees must clock in upon entry and clock out upon departure using the reception kiosk tablet or office camera terminal.
  • The facial recognition terminal enforces anti-spoofing liveness verification. Still photos, screen replays, or proxy attempts are flagged as severe misconduct.
  • No raw photographs are stored; verification relies on bank-grade 128-dimensional encrypted mathematical vector embeddings.
4. Remote & Field Staff GPS Geofencing

Employees authorized to work remotely or in field roles (sales, client site maintenance) must check in using the Curiosity HUB mobile self-service portal. Clock-ins are automatically validated against predefined GPS geofenced coordinates.

5. Grace Period & Late Marks
  • Grace Window: A 15-minute grace period is granted (up to 09:45 AM for General Shift).
  • Late Arrival: Clocking in between 16 and 60 minutes after shift start is marked as LATE. Three (3) unexcused late marks in a single calendar month result in a half-day pay deduction.
  • Half-Day Threshold: Arriving more than 60 minutes late, or departing more than 60 minutes before shift completion without prior managerial consent, is logged as a half-day.
6. Leave Application & Prior Approval
  • Planned leaves (Casual/Earned) must be submitted via the Employee Self-Service Portal at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Unforeseen sick leaves must be notified to the reporting manager within two (2) hours of shift commencement.
  • Absences exceeding two (2) consecutive days without notification are treated as unauthorized absence (Loss of Pay).
7. Payroll Synchronization

Daily biometric records and approved leave requests are synchronized directly into the Automated Payroll Engine on the 26th of each month. Verified payable days dictate gross salary, overtime payouts, and statutory compliance deductions (PF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS).
