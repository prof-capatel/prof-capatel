Real-Time Leave Balances & Ledgers

Employees can check their Casual, Sick, and Earned leave quotas anytime on mobile or desktop. Balances update immediately when leaves are taken or accrued at month-end.

  • Configurable annual leave entitlements
  • Transparent ledger of past approvals
  • Pro-rata auto-accrual for new joiners

Multi-Tier Manager Approvals

Route leave requests to reporting managers, department heads, or HR admins based on custom hierarchy. Approvers receive instant notifications and can approve or reject with 1 click.
