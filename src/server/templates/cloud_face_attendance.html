How It Works

State-of-the-Art Biometrics Built for the Cloud

Enterprise-grade facial recognition engineered for instantaneous clock-ins across corporate offices, factories, retail chains, and schools.

128-Dimensional Vector AI

Powered by dlib ResNet Euclidean distance matching with in-memory RAM matrix caching. Generates immutable mathematical vector embeddings rather than storing raw photos.

  • Irreversible facial vector arrays
  • Multi-angle face matching (Front/Left/Right)
  • Sub-250ms verification benchmark

Multi-Stage Anti-Spoofing

Prevents buddy punching and fraudulent clock-ins. Our vision pipeline inspects specular reflections, 2D FFT spectral texture frequency, and micro-motion across frames.

  • Rejects photos and printed masks
  • Blocks smartphone screen replays
  • Audit log snapshot with timestamp

Zero-Hardware Kiosk Setup

No specialized biometric readers to purchase or maintain. Run our touchless attendance kiosk on any browser-enabled iPad, tablet, laptop, or mobile device.

  • 5-minute setup with zero technician visits
  • Auto-caching during internet dropouts
  • Central real-time multi-location sync
