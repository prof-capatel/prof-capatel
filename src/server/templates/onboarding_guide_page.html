Administrator Playbook

Curiosity HUB SaaS Quick Onboarding Guide

Follow this comprehensive 10-step administrative guide to configure organizational settings, custom branding, rotational work shifts, GPS geofencing, employee facial vector enrollment, and automated payroll sync.

Step 0 • 1 Min Initial Access

Prerequisite & Administrator Login

Log in to your dedicated company portal (/login or custom organization slug) using your administrator credentials provided during account provisioning.

  • Open your organization's custom login gateway URL.
  • Authenticate with the administrator email and secure password.
  • Verify the tenant branding badge and organization name in the top navigation bar.
Step 1 • 2 Mins Foundation

Organization Settings & Branch Locations

Establish corporate identity, upload your official logo, and register all office locations with exact coordinates for tamper-proof GPS mobile geofencing.

  • Navigate to Settings → Organization Profile.
  • Upload company logo to display on payslips, portals, and kiosk terminals.
  • Add branch office locations with latitude, longitude, and geofence radius.
Step 2 • 1 Min Branding

Themes & Corporate Appearance Customization

Customize your workspace aesthetics with custom corporate colors and theme presets (Clean Light, Modern Dark, or Warm Academic).

  • Open Settings → Themes & Appearance.
  • Select a curated theme preset or enter custom brand hex color codes.
  • Save settings; styling automatically propagates across kiosks and employee portals.
Step 3 • 2 Mins Structure

Departments & Designation Hierarchy

Define corporate functional divisions (Engineering, HR, Sales, Operations) and formal designations linked to statutory salary templates.

  • Create operational departments with unique departmental codes.
  • Add job designations and link them to default salary compensation structures.
  • Enable automated permission grouping based on designation level.
Step 4 • 2 Mins Schedules

Work Shifts, Grace Periods & Geofencing Rules

Configure rotational work schedules, morning/evening shifts, grace period minutes, and strict GPS perimeter boundaries for field employees.

  • Specify shift start times, end times, and lunch break durations.
  • Set grace arrival minutes before automatic 'LATE' flag is triggered.
  • Configure maximum allowed geofence drift radius in meters (50m - 200m).
Step 5 • 3 Mins Biometrics

Employee Facial Vector Enrollment

Enroll staff in seconds via webcam kiosk or dispatch single-use mobile enrollment tokens allowing employees to capture face vectors directly from their smartphone.

  • Import bulk employee roster via standard CSV spreadsheet.
  • Capture Frontal, Left, and Right angle vectors for sub-250ms recognition accuracy.
  • Raw photographs can be immediately deleted; only non-reversible 128-d vectors are preserved for privacy.
Step 6 • 1 Min Directory

Employee Directory & Status Management

Maintain real-time employee directories, monitor facial encoding completion rates, manage active/inactive statuses, and review banking and statutory details.

  • Filter personnel by branch office, department, and work shift.
  • Re-enroll or update face samples if appearance changes occur.
  • Export directory lists to Excel / CSV with one click.
Step 7 • 1 Min Live Operations

Live Biometric Kiosk & Touchless Clock-In

Launch full-screen attendance terminals on reception laptops, mounted tablets, or iPad kiosks for instant touchless clock-ins under 250 milliseconds.

  • Deploy with standard HD USB webcams or integrated tablet cameras.
  • Temporal anti-spoof liveness gating blocks photos, videos, and phone screens.
  • Works reliably offline with local vector caching and auto-sync when online.
Step 8 • 1 Min Auditing

Real-Time Attendance Logs & Audit Trails

Audit real-time verification logs with clock-in timestamps, verification methods (WEBCAM, MOBILE_GPS, GEOFENCE), and matched confidence Euclidean scores.

  • Complete tamper-proof audit trails with soft-delete protections.
  • Instant CSV and Excel attendance reports filtered by date range.
  • Automated daily absence notifications sent to departmental managers.
Step 9 • 2 Mins Compensation

Leave Management & Automated Payroll Processing

Attendance logs link seamlessly with approved leaves, automatically calculating statutory PF, ESIC, Professional Tax, and Loss of Pay (LOP) deductions for downloadable PDF payslips.

  • 1-click batch payroll execution for all branches.
  • Automated payslip PDF generation with custom company branding and QR verification.
  • Direct bank advice export for automated salary disbursement via NEFT/RTGS.
