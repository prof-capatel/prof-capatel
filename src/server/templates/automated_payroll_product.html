Payroll Architecture

Built to Eliminate Payroll Calculation Errors & Delays

A clean, transparent, compliant payroll engine built specifically for Indian SMEs, retail chains, and enterprise teams.

Attendance-Driven Calculations

No spreadsheets or manual attendance exports. The engine reads verified facial biometric clock-ins, calculates total payable days, auto-deducts late arrivals or unapproved leaves, and credits approved overtime.

  • Auto-detection of half-days and late marks
  • Overtime pay based on hourly rate rules
  • Automatic Loss of Pay (LOP) calculations

Statutory Compliance (PF, ESI & PT)

Pre-configured with standard Indian statutory deductions. Automatically deducts Employee Provident Fund (PF at 12%), Employee State Insurance (ESI at 0.75%), and state-specific Professional Tax (PT).

  • PF statutory wage ceiling caps (₹15,000)
  • ESI eligibility wage limit checks (₹21,000)
  • Gujarat, Maharashtra & Pan-India PT slabs

Professional PDF Payslips

Generate branded, audit-ready PDF payslips with 1 click. Features company logo, breakdown of earnings (Basic, HRA, DA) and deductions (PF, PT, TDS), net pay in words, and employee download portal.

  • Indian currency number-to-words generator
  • Bank transfer CSV export ready
  • Employee self-service mobile download
